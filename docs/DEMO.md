## A brief to try

> Plan a community garden celebration in Jaipur for 80 people, with a ₹1,20,000 budget, on a future date. Use seasonal local food, reusable decor, wheelchair-accessible paths and seating, and an activity for children. Keep the mood relaxed and welcoming.

Choose an actual future date in the form. If Google quota is unavailable, show the labeled sample and explain that live generation was unavailable; do not describe a sample as AI output.

## Before recording

- Verify the live link and selected model using your own key.
- Use fictional event data; avoid private guest names, contact details, and payment information.
- Start from a clean browser-local plan if you want to avoid showing unrelated events.
- Mention cloud account saving only if you have configured and verified it.
- Confirm the public repository and video are accessible while signed out.
